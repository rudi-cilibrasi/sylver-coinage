Parallel periodicity block: the row-409 closure is measured, and it is vast
2026-08-19 to 2026-08-20 (~21 h wall clock across three launches; cumulative
campaign total ~501 h)

Context:
  After the even flank of X = {16,26,82,88} closed all-N
  (RUN_X_EVEN_FLANK.txt), the engine gained batch-parallel exact
  fallbacks and root-level hints (--exact-threads; commits 4e14000,
  115b917, 4ce3c5b) and resumed the saved 220,574-shape row-409
  frontier.  All ten periodicity tests, an ASan/UBSan interrupt-resume
  control under threads, and an isolated-copy resume of the real
  rowstate passed before launch.

Launch 1 (~7 h, 8 threads): livelock, diagnosed and fixed.
  Collect mode correctly refused to memoize unknown values, so every
  sweep entry re-derived the entire unknown region down to the same
  pending misses: 12,473,707,518 evaluations, shapes 220,574 -> 223,185,
  zero batches.  SIGINT saved cleanly.  Fix: a transient per-sweep
  unknown-memo, cleared before each re-sweep, never checkpointed, never
  an outcome (commit 115b917).  The thrash log is preserved as
  move26_data/periodicity_parallel_thrash.log.

Launch 2 (~7 h, 8 threads): healthy expansion to 1,406,524 shapes.
  Evaluations 12.47e9 -> 23.22e9; even parts 447 -> 1,051.  Stopped by
  SIGINT on memory projection; 3.86 GB rowstate saved.  A batch-pending
  threshold was added so early exact batches could prune expansion via
  first_p reset flags (commit 4ce3c5b).

Launch 3 (~7 h, 6 threads, threshold 20,000): the measurement.
  Expansion continued to 3,157,209 shapes and 1,465 even parts
  (evaluations 23.22e9 -> 50.29e9) with RSS tracking ~11-13 KB/shape.
  A 42 GB guard sent SIGINT; at that moment the sweep had truncated
  with **pending=12,541,230 distinct base-region exact positions** (the
  per-sid threshold check could not see inside one deep recursion; a
  4x-threshold cap now bounds this).  The 8.7 GB rowstate saved
  atomically.

Soundness audit finding (the important one):
  Tracing 19 unexplained new cache rows exposed that
  ensure_single_history discarded evaluate's return value.  In collect
  mode evaluate may return unknown; history_to nevertheless advanced,
  permanently marking a singleton history complete while missing
  values.  A missed P would corrupt the first_p reset flag, which the
  recurrence's *outcomes* depend on.  Serial mode is unaffected (inline
  solves cannot be unknown).  Because launches 1-3 registered 1,018 new
  even parts whose fresh histories swept under collect mode, all three
  parallel rowstates are considered tainted and were discarded.  The
  engine now stops history advancement at an unknown and defers the
  dependent child as unknown for the sweep.
  The 19 cache rows themselves are sound (solved inline during
  checkpoint-load validation with collect mode off); two were
  independently re-verified with the standalone native solver:
    {16,26,38,61,82,88,123,127}  N (winner 11,     17,262 states)
    {16,26,38,82,88,97,125,127}  N (winner 41,  5,439,343 states)

State after the block (all audited):

    (220,574 shapes, evaluations 22,641,268)
  exact cache: 216,986 rows (216,967 post-flank + 19 verified), no

What the block established:
  1.  Row X+409's dependency closure exceeds 3.15 million translated
      shapes and 12.5 million distinct base-region exact positions —
      at least 15x beyond everything the 480-hour serial campaign
      reached, with expansion still unsaturated at the 45 GB memory
      guard.  This is the first quantification of why the serial siege
      never completed the row.
  2.  The recycled exact cache absorbs nearly all base-region demand
      during expansion: the binding constraint on this machine is
      memory for the shape graph, not CPU for exact solves.
  3.  Completing row 409 by brute closure requires order 10^7 exact
      solves plus a shape graph beyond 62 GB under the present
      representation.  On this machine that path needs either a
      substantially more compact representation or a different
      mathematical approach to the tail.

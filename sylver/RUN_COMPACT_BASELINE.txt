Plan adjustment from measurement:
  transitions (64%) and the row memo (21%) dominate.  The row memo was
  not in the original spec; it joins Task 6 as a flat open-addressing
  table (U64 -> int8, insert-only per row, cleared at row boundaries —
  the exact contract the unordered_map provides today), because unlike
  every other subsystem it scales with active-row work rather than
  shape count and would otherwise dominate at 3M+ shapes.

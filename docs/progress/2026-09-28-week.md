## U and X

- **U.** The September 5 report reduced U P to "X N and Q N", resting on the
  July audit of U's other branches. PR #31 checks that premise obligation
  by obligation: 52 Book certificates, one finite witness with native and
  Python replays (move 70 by 261; 201,555,961 states), the certified nodes
  G, F and V for moves 8, 12, 36 and 56, and Q through W. Only move 82, to X,
  is open. The Book's replay counts match exactly both Python
  reproductions that the July record kept.
- **X.** Every even reply to X is N (Attempt 20), so X is N exactly when
  some odd reply reaches a P-position. Verified shared-memo sweeps
  (PRs #31–#33) classify every odd reply from 409 to 687 as N. Reply 683
  alone needed 620 million states, and the memo's next growth step would
  need about 72 GB, more than this 62 GB host has, so further replies need
  a leaner memo or a larger machine. The first open reply is 689.

## The arena

- **Proof-search arena** (#14; issues #6–#12): a frozen, content-addressed
  snapshot of 308,280 facts; a closed proof language with a fixed verifier;
  accounted episodes scored S = (C+100)·(CPU+1); baseline policies,
  bounded policy evolution and CLI tournaments; an offline pilot with
  144 of 144 valid episodes.
- **Accounting and sandbox** (#15): incremental process-tree CPU accounting,
  and a Landlock and seccomp sandbox for hosts without user namespaces.
- **Certificate golf and The Book** (#19; issue #16): programs certify
  public results from an untrusted hint database. The Book admits every
  distinct valid certificate and marks each target's cheapest to check as
  its record. It now holds 127 certificates for 102 targets, covering 50 of
  W's 52 obligations (#19, #25, #28, #31) and 52 of U's 59 (#31).
  `book admit` (#31) admits curator proofs after the Book's own replay, and
  [the Book page](https://rudi-cilibrasi.github.io/sylver-coinage/book.html)
  (#23) replays small certificates in the browser.
- **Game arena** (#20, #22, #29; issue #17): programs play Sylver Coinage
  under CPU clocks in sandboxed leagues, with Bradley–Terry ratings and
  blunder analysis. A 180-game pilot league of the four built-in players
  is recorded. Game results are not proofs. The book player now answers Q
  with 62.

In Sylver Coinage, W is the position after the numbers 16, 26, 62 and 98 have been named. Every number W generates is even, so infinitely many moves remain; but W is short, so by the Quiet End Theorem it is a P-position (the player to move loses) exactly when each of its 52 obligations, the moves listed below, leads to an N-position.
